Cue · Citron direction 01 · Philosophy
Philosophy

Pre-production should feel like waking up, not like filing taxes.

Cue is saturated, certain, and loud. It treats the master breakdown as a living document rather than a spreadsheet to be tolerated. Every decision in this book bends toward one purpose — make production people feel that the tool is on their side. Six emotions, no more. Color as signal, not decoration. Type that sounds like a human, not a template.

Cue · Citron direction 04 · Wordmark
Wordmark

The wordmark is the brand.
No glyph, no stripboard, no crest.

cue.
Case & weight

Always lowercase. Always Bricolage Grotesque Bold (700). Never italic. Coral dot is optional — use it only when the wordmark stands alone as a product moment.

Spacing

Letter-spacing −0.06em. Baseline-clear margin equal to the cap height. Never anchor to the corner of a layout — the wordmark needs to breathe.

Colour pairings

Midnight on Citron, Citron on Midnight, Midnight on Bone. Coral is for emphasis only. Never outline, never gradient, never drop-shadow.
